## 9Proxy's two models, and why the difference matters

9Proxy sells residential IPs two ways, and picking the wrong one is the most common way to waste money.

### Residential by IP — fixed IPs, unlimited bandwidth

You buy a number of IPs. Each one stays alive for a few hours up to roughly 24 hours, and while it is active you can push as much data through it as you like. There is no traffic counter. Unused IPs never expire, so a pack bought this month can still be sitting in your balance next month.

This suits work where sessions have to stay stable: logged-in accounts, checkout or form flows, anything where the site notices a mid-session IP change. The trade-off is setup: IP-based proxies need the 9Proxy desktop app, which forwards local ports on your machine, and the IPs do not rotate on their own — you rotate them yourself, or use the auto-rotation ports.

### Residential by GB — pay for traffic, generate unlimited endpoints

Here you buy bandwidth instead of IPs. You can generate as many proxy endpoints as you want, target by country, state, city, ZIP or ISP, and choose sticky sessions (same IP for X minutes) or rotating (new IP per request). Everything runs from the dashboard, no app required, and authentication is either username/password or IP whitelisting. GB packages carry 180 days of validity; the Enterprise tiers remove the expiry entirely.

This suits scraping, ad verification, geo-checks, price monitoring and anything that rotates across many IPs. It is also the model that behaves better when you are not sure how much traffic a project will burn.

Cost-saving details worth knowing: the Today List lets you reuse any IP from the previous 24 hours at no extra charge, which 9Proxy estimates trims roughly 20–30% off a recurring workload, and auto-refresh replaces IPs that go offline within about a minute so a dead exit node does not stall a running job.
